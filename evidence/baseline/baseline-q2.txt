Based on the documents provided, the answer depends on your policy type and the date of your loss:

## For Personal Auto Policies with Endorsement ACME-END-RR:

**For losses on or after May 1, 2026:**
- **$45 per day**
- **30 days maximum** per loss
- **$1,350 maximum** per loss

**For losses before May 1, 2026:**
- **$30 per day**
- **30 days maximum** per loss
- **$900 maximum** per loss

The actual reimbursement is the lesser of your actual daily rental charge or the per-day maximum.

## For Commercial Fleet Policies:

Fleet vehicles don't use rental reimbursement. Instead, they have **downtime reimbursement** which covers:
- **$75 per day**
- **45 days maximum**

**Note:** To confirm whether you have rental coverage, check your policy declarations page or contact your adjuster. Rental coverage only applies if you have the rental reimbursement endorsement attached to your policy.
