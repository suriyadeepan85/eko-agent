# Reference — Direct Repair Program (DRP) Rules
**Owner:** Claims Operations | **Effective:** 2025-07-01

## Insured's right to choose
The insured may select any licensed repair facility. The carrier may not require use of a network shop as a condition of payment.

## What the network adds
Where the insured selects a DRP facility:
- Repairs carry a lifetime workmanship guarantee administered by the carrier.
- The shop writes the estimate directly, removing the wait for a carrier inspection.
- Supplements are approved by the shop up to $1,500 without adjuster review.

## Non-network repairs
Where the insured selects a non-network facility:
- The carrier writes the estimate based on inspection or photos.
- Payment is based on prevailing competitive labor rates for the repair area. Any amount the shop charges above that rate is the insured's responsibility unless the carrier agrees in writing.
- Supplements require adjuster review before authorization.

## Parts
Estimates may specify aftermarket or recycled parts of like kind and quality on vehicles older than three model years, subject to state amendment restrictions.
