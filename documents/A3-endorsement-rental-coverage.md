# Endorsement ACME-END-RR — Rental Reimbursement
**Attaches to:** ACME-PA-2025 personal auto form | **Effective:** 2025-07-01

This endorsement adds rental reimbursement to a personal auto policy when the covered auto is disabled by a loss payable under collision or comprehensive.

## What triggers coverage
Rental reimbursement begins when the covered auto is disabled for more than 24 consecutive hours as a result of a covered physical damage loss, or immediately upon a total loss determination.

## What is paid
The carrier reimburses the actual cost of a comparable rental vehicle, subject to the per-day and per-loss maximums published in the rental reimbursement limits schedule.

## What is not paid
- Fuel, tolls, insurance sold by the rental agency, and upgrade charges above a comparable class.
- Rental days after the covered auto is repaired and available, or after the total loss settlement offer is issued, whichever is earlier.
- Rentals arising from mechanical breakdown, routine maintenance, or a loss not payable under the policy.

## Interaction with fleet
This endorsement cannot be attached to the commercial fleet form. Fleet vehicles use downtime reimbursement instead.
