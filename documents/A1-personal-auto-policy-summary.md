# Acme Mutual — Personal Auto Policy Summary
**Form:** ACME-PA-2025 | **Effective:** 2025-07-01

This summary describes coverage under the personal auto form for private passenger vehicles owned or leased by the named insured.

## Covered vehicles
A covered auto is a private passenger car, pickup, or van shown on the declarations page, plus any newly acquired auto of the same type for 14 days after acquisition.

## Coverage parts
- **Liability** — bodily injury and property damage to others. Standard limit 100/300/50.
- **Collision** — damage to the covered auto from impact with another object or overturn. Subject to the collision deductible.
- **Comprehensive** — loss from causes other than collision, including fire, theft, hail, falling objects, and glass breakage. Subject to the comprehensive deductible.
- **Medical payments** — up to $5,000 per person.
- **Uninsured/underinsured motorist** — matches liability limits unless rejected in writing.

## Settlement basis
Physical damage losses are settled at actual cash value (ACV) at the time of loss, less the applicable deductible. Where repair is elected, the carrier pays the lesser of repair cost or ACV.

## Rental
Rental reimbursement is not included in the base form. It is available by endorsement — see the rental reimbursement endorsement.

## Territory
Coverage applies in the United States, its territories, and Canada.
