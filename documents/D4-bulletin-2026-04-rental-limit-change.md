# Claims Bulletin 2026-04 — Rental Reimbursement Limit Increase
**Issued:** 2026-04-15 | **Effective for losses on or after:** 2026-05-01

## Summary
The per-day rental reimbursement maximum under endorsement ACME-END-RR is increased from $30 to **$45 per day**. The maximum number of days per loss is unchanged at 30. The resulting maximum per loss becomes **$1,350**.

## Why
Market rental rates have risen materially since the schedule was last set in 2025. Reimbursement at $30 per day no longer covers a comparable class vehicle in most markets.

## Scope
- Applies to losses with a date of loss on or after 2026-05-01.
- Losses before that date continue at the prior limit.
- The rental reimbursement limits schedule will be reissued at the next product release. **Until then, this bulletin controls.**

## Action required
Handlers must not quote the per-day figure from the existing limits schedule for new losses. Rental authorizations already issued at the prior rate are not retroactively adjusted.
