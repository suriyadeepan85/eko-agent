# Reference — Rental Reimbursement Limits Schedule
**Owner:** Product | **Effective:** 2025-07-01

Applies to personal auto policies with endorsement ACME-END-RR attached.

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Per day maximum | **$30** |
| Maximum days per loss | **30** |
| Maximum per loss | **$900** |

## Notes
- Reimbursement is the lesser of the actual daily rental charge or the per-day maximum.
- The day count begins on the day the covered auto becomes disabled, or the day of the total loss determination.
- The day count ends when repairs are complete or on the date the total loss offer is issued, whichever is earlier, regardless of days remaining.
- Fleet vehicles do not use this schedule. See the commercial fleet form for downtime reimbursement.
