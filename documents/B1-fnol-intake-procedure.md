# Claims Procedure — First Notice of Loss (FNOL) Intake
**Owner:** Claims Operations | **Version:** 3.1 | **Effective:** 2025-09-15

## Purpose
Standardize how a new auto loss is captured so that downstream assignment and valuation have complete information.

## Required fields at intake
1. Policy number and named insured verification.
2. Date, time, and location of loss.
3. Description of loss in the reporting party's own words.
4. Vehicle year, make, model, VIN, and current drivability.
5. Other parties, vehicles, injuries, and police report number if any.
6. Whether the vehicle is currently at a repair facility, tow yard, or the insured's residence.

## Coverage screening
The intake handler confirms which coverage parts are in force at the date of loss. Where rental reimbursement appears on the declarations, the handler advises the caller that rental eligibility is determined once the vehicle is confirmed disabled.

## Drivability question
If the reporting party states the vehicle is not drivable, the file is flagged **non-drivable** at intake. Non-drivable files route to expedited assignment.

## Output
FNOL produces a claim number and a completed intake record. Assignment to an adjuster is handled under the adjuster assignment guideline, not at intake.
