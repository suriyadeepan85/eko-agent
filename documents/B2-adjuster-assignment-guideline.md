# Claims Procedure — Adjuster Assignment and Contact Standards
**Owner:** Claims Operations | **Version:** 2.4 | **Effective:** 2025-09-15

## Assignment
Claims are assigned by segment:
- **Express** — single vehicle, drivable, no injuries, estimated damage under $3,500. Handled by a desk adjuster.
- **Standard** — drivable with damage of $3,500 or more, or any multi-vehicle loss without injury. Field adjuster assigned.
- **Complex** — injuries, suspected total loss, commercial fleet vehicles, or any file with a fraud flag. Senior field adjuster assigned.

## Contact standards (normal operations)
- Express and Standard: the assigned adjuster makes first contact with the insured within **one business day** of assignment.
- Complex: first contact within **one business day**, plus a documented file plan within three business days.
- Non-drivable files: contact within **four hours** during business hours.

## Inspection
Physical inspection is scheduled within three business days of first contact for Standard and Complex files. Express files may be settled on photo estimate.

## Catastrophe conditions
These contact standards apply to normal operations only. When a catastrophe event is declared, the timelines in the catastrophe surge rules replace the standards in this section.
