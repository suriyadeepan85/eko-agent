# Claims Procedure — Total Loss Valuation
**Owner:** Claims Operations | **Version:** 4.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01

## When a vehicle is a total loss
A vehicle is declared a total loss when the estimated cost of repair plus anticipated supplements equals or exceeds **75%** of the vehicle's pre-loss actual cash value. Files at or above this ratio are moved to the total loss unit.

## Establishing actual cash value
ACV is established from a market valuation report using comparable vehicles sold or listed within the preceding 90 days, within a 100 mile radius, adjusted for mileage, options, and condition. A minimum of three comparables is required.

## Settlement calculation
Settlement equals ACV, plus applicable taxes and fees where required by state amendment, less the applicable deductible, less any prior unrepaired damage adjustment. Salvage is retained by the carrier unless the insured elects owner retention.

## Owner retention
An insured electing to retain salvage receives the settlement less the salvage bid value. Branded title requirements are disclosed in writing.

## State variation
Where a state amendment specifies a different threshold or a different settlement component, the state amendment governs.
