# Acme Mutual — Commercial Fleet Auto Policy
**Form:** ACME-CF-2025 | **Effective:** 2025-07-01

This form covers vehicles owned, hired, or borrowed by a business entity and scheduled on the fleet declarations.

## Covered autos
Any vehicle titled to the named business entity, including light commercial vans, box trucks under 26,000 lbs GVWR, and passenger vehicles used for business purposes. Employee-owned vehicles used for business are covered under hired and non-owned liability only.

## Coverage parts
- **Liability** — combined single limit of $1,000,000 per accident. There is no split 100/300/50 structure on this form.
- **Physical damage** — collision and comprehensive, settled at ACV less the fleet deductible.
- **Hired and non-owned auto liability** — applies to vehicles the entity does not own.
- **Downtime reimbursement** — loss-of-use payments for a disabled scheduled vehicle, up to $75 per day for 45 days. This replaces personal rental reimbursement; the personal auto rental endorsement does not apply to fleet vehicles.

## Deductibles
Fleet deductibles are set per schedule, commonly $1,000 collision and $1,000 comprehensive. See the deductible table for defaults.

## Driver eligibility
Only drivers listed on the approved driver schedule are covered. Claims involving unlisted drivers are referred to underwriting before payment.
