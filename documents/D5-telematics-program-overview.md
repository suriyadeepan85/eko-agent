# Program Overview — Acme DriveSense Telematics
**Owner:** Product | **Effective:** 2025-07-01

DriveSense is an optional usage-based program for personal auto policyholders.

## How it works
The insured enrolls through the mobile app, which records trip data using the phone's sensors. A scoring period of 90 days establishes a driving score based on hard braking, rapid acceleration, night driving hours, and handheld phone use while moving.

## Discounts
- Enrollment discount of 5% applied immediately at enrollment.
- Renewal discount from 0% to 30% based on the driving score at the end of the scoring period.
- The renewal discount is recalculated at each renewal using the most recent 90 days.

## Data use
Trip data is used for rating only. It is not used to determine fault in a claim and is not shared with third parties except as required by law.

## Withdrawal
The insured may withdraw at any time. The enrollment discount is removed at the next renewal. Score data is retained for 24 months.

## Eligibility
Available on personal auto policies only. Not available on commercial fleet policies.
