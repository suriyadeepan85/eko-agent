# Claims Procedure — Subrogation Checklist
**Owner:** Recovery Unit | **Version:** 1.8 | **Effective:** 2025-11-01

Subrogation is pursued where another party is wholly or partly responsible for a loss the carrier has paid.

## Referral triggers
- Rear-end impact where the insured was struck.
- Any loss with an identified adverse carrier and a police report indicating fault.
- Property damage caused by a commercial entity, including repair facilities and tow operators.

## Checklist before referral
1. Payment has been issued to the insured and the deductible amount is documented.
2. Adverse party details, carrier, and claim number are recorded.
3. Photographs, estimate, and any police report are attached.
4. Statement from the insured is on file.

## Deductible recovery
Where recovery is obtained, the insured's deductible is reimbursed pro rata before the carrier retains the balance. The insured is notified of the pro rata basis in writing.

## Time limits
Referral is made within 30 days of first payment. Statutes of limitation vary by state; the recovery unit tracks the applicable date on each file.
