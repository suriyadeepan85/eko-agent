# Reference — Glass Claim Handling
**Owner:** Claims Operations | **Effective:** 2025-07-01

## Scope
Covers windshield and other auto glass damage reported under comprehensive coverage.

## Repair versus replacement
Chips smaller than a quarter and cracks under six inches outside the driver's primary viewing area are repaired rather than replaced where the glass vendor confirms repairability.

## Deductible treatment
Glass losses are subject to the comprehensive deductible shown on the declarations page. **Repair-only glass claims are processed with the deductible waived** where the vendor performs a repair rather than a replacement. Replacement glass claims take the full comprehensive deductible.

## Vendor routing
Glass claims are routed to the glass vendor network directly from intake and do not require adjuster assignment. Calibration of driver assistance cameras after windshield replacement is covered as part of the glass loss.

## Reporting
A repaired glass claim is recorded as a comprehensive loss on the claim history even where no payment net of deductible is made.
