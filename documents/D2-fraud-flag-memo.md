# Internal Memo — Fraud Indicators and File Flagging
**From:** Special Investigations Unit (SIU) | **Date:** 2025-10-20

## Purpose
Clarify when a claims handler flags a file for SIU review and what happens after.

## Indicators
Any two of the following prompt a flag:
- Loss reported more than 14 days after the date of loss with no explanation.
- Policy inception within 30 days of the date of loss.
- No police report on a claimed theft or hit-and-run.
- Prior unrepaired damage in the same impact area.
- Reluctance to permit inspection, or vehicle unavailable at the stated location.

## What flagging does
Flagging routes the file to Complex segment and assigns a senior field adjuster. Payment authority is suspended pending SIU review. The insured is not told the file is under SIU review; they are told the file is under standard review.

## Decision authority
Only the SIU manager may clear or extend a flag. The handling adjuster cannot clear a flag. Denial recommendations arising from SIU findings require sign-off from the Claims Executive.

## Timeline
SIU issues an initial disposition within 15 business days of flagging.
