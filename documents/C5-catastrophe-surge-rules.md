# Reference — Catastrophe Surge Operating Rules
**Owner:** Claims Operations | **Effective:** 2025-07-01

## Declaration
A catastrophe (CAT) event is declared by the Claims Executive when a single weather or other event is expected to generate more than 250 claims in a defined geography. A CAT code is assigned and applied to all related claims.

## Contact standards under CAT
While a CAT declaration is active, the normal contact standards in the adjuster assignment guideline are replaced by:
- First contact with the insured within **five business days** of assignment.
- Inspection scheduled within **ten business days** of first contact.
- Non-drivable and injury files retain priority routing and are contacted within one business day.

## Handling changes
- Photo and virtual estimating are the default for drivable vehicles.
- Hail-only claims under $5,000 may be settled without physical inspection.
- Supplement authority for network shops is raised to $3,000 during the declaration.

## Deactivation
Standard timelines resume 30 days after the CAT declaration is lifted, or earlier at the discretion of Claims Operations.
