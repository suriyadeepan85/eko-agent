# Claims Procedure — Total Loss Valuation
**Owner:** Claims Operations | **Version:** 3.2 | **Effective:** 2023-03-01

## When a vehicle is a total loss
A vehicle is declared a total loss when the estimated cost of repair plus anticipated supplements equals or exceeds **70%** of the vehicle's pre-loss actual cash value.

## Establishing actual cash value
ACV is established from a market valuation report using comparable vehicles sold or listed within the preceding 180 days, within a 150 mile radius. A minimum of two comparables is required.

## Settlement calculation
Settlement equals ACV less the applicable deductible, less any prior unrepaired damage adjustment. Salvage is retained by the carrier unless the insured elects owner retention.

## Owner retention
An insured electing to retain salvage receives the settlement less the salvage bid value.

## Documentation
The valuation report and the adjuster's condition notes are attached to the file before an offer is extended.
