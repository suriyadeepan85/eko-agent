# Acme Mutual — Physical Damage Exclusions
**Applies to:** ACME-PA-2025 and ACME-CF-2025 | **Effective:** 2025-07-01

The carrier does not pay for loss under collision or comprehensive coverage in the following circumstances.

- Loss occurring while the covered auto is used to carry persons or property for a fee, unless a ridesharing endorsement is attached.
- Loss caused by war, nuclear hazard, or governmental seizure.
- Wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown, and road damage to tires, unless the loss results from a covered peril.
- Loss to electronic equipment not permanently installed in the covered auto.
- Loss to custom furnishings or equipment in a pickup or van, unless declared and scheduled.
- Loss occurring while the covered auto is operated in a racing or speed contest.
- Loss arising from intentional acts by or at the direction of an insured.
- Destruction or confiscation by government authority for law enforcement purposes.

## Note on non-covered vehicle types
Vehicles not eligible for these forms are listed on the declarations page under "ineligible risks." Eligibility questions are directed to underwriting, not to claims.
