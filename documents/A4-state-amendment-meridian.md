# State Amendment — Meridian
**Amends:** ACME-PA-2025 and ACME-CF-2025 | **Effective:** 2026-02-01

The following provisions apply to policies covering vehicles principally garaged in the State of Meridian and supersede conflicting provisions in the base form or in claims procedure documents.

## Total loss threshold
For vehicles garaged in Meridian, a vehicle is declared a total loss only where the sum of repair cost and salvage value equals or exceeds **100%** of the vehicle's pre-loss actual cash value. The percentage thresholds used in the carrier's general total loss valuation procedure do not apply to Meridian risks.

## Appraisal rights
If the insured and the carrier disagree on the amount of loss, either party may demand appraisal in writing. Each party selects an appraiser; the appraisers select an umpire. The written agreement of any two is binding as to the amount of loss.

## Sales tax and fees
Total loss settlements on Meridian risks must include applicable sales tax and title transfer fees in the settlement amount, whether or not the insured purchases a replacement vehicle.

## Notice period
The carrier must provide 30 days written notice before non-renewal on Meridian personal auto policies.
