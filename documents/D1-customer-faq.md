# Customer FAQ — After You Report an Auto Claim
**Owner:** Customer Communications | **Last reviewed:** 2025-08-01

**How soon will someone call me?**
An adjuster will contact you within 24 hours of your claim being filed.

**Will I have to pay a deductible?**
Yes, your deductible is subtracted from what we pay you. You can find it on your declarations page.

**Can I pick my own repair shop?**
Yes. You can use any licensed shop you like. Using a shop in our network means we guarantee the work for as long as you own the car.

**What if my car is written off?**
If your car cannot be repaired economically, we pay you what it was worth just before the accident, minus your deductible.

**Do I get a rental car?**
If you have rental coverage on your policy, we cover a rental while your car is being fixed, up to the daily limit on your policy.

**How long do I have to report a claim?**
Report as soon as you reasonably can. Late reporting can affect how we handle your claim.
